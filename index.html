The Friction Index

Every time Bharat (India) falls behind a technology wave, the debate is loud — but vague. This is the opposite. A concrete, sourced map of the rules, regulators and paperwork that slow builders down.

Most people who complain that “the system is rigged” can’t name the rule, the form, or the office responsible. This dashboard does. It catalogues 56 documented friction points across the life of an Indian company — from incorporation to raising capital to exporting to shutting down — and for each one shows what the rule is, which body enforces it, how it bites, and what it costs. Filter it, search it, and follow the sources. Nothing here is a slogan; every card links to its evidence.

What this is

A public, sourced catalogue of the regulatory and administrative friction that slows innovation in Bharat (India). It exists because the debate about Bharat (India) “catching up” is usually conducted in slogans, while the actual bottlenecks are specific, nameable and fixable — a form here, a threshold there, a body that takes months to say yes.

The framing is simple: if the goal is more builders, capital and risk-takers staying and compounding in Bharat (India), the state’s highest-value job is to lower friction — not to pick winners. This map is the evidence base for that argument.

Method & limits

Compiled from government notifications, PIB releases, regulator circulars, court orders, parliamentary answers and reporting, each linked on its card. Figures reflect the most recent information captured at the time of research (2025–2026).

This is a research compilation, not legal or tax advice, and rules change often — verify the current position with a qualified professional before acting. Severity ratings are an editorial judgement about impact on early-stage builders.
